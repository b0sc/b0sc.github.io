# Free AI for Students: Gemini and Beyond 🎓🤖

This guide covers:

1. **Google Gemini's student plan**: what you get and how to claim it, step by step
2. **Other AI tools** with free student access or free API tokens, and how to get each one
3. A **comparison table** to help you choose

**Checked on 7 October 2026.** These offers change often. Each section links to the official page, so check it before you sign up.

## Part 1: Google Gemini's Free Student Plan

### What is the offer?

Google is giving **eligible university students a free year of Google AI Plus**, its paid Gemini plan. In the US, students get the higher **Google AI Pro** plan instead. Nepal and more than 140 other countries get **AI Plus**.

![Gemini student offer page](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/gemini-student-page.png)

### What's included in the AI Plus student plan

According to Google's official student page, the free plan includes:

- **2× higher usage limits** than the free version of Gemini
- **400 GB of cloud storage** across Google Drive, Gmail and Photos
- **Gemini Omni**, Google's newest model, including **video generation**
- **Unlimited uploads** for Study Notebooks, Visuals and Gemini Live

### Features that help with studying

![Gemini personalised study tools](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/gemini-study-tools.png)

- **Study Notebooks**: upload your lecture notes, slides or PDFs. Gemini turns them into a study notebook with a learning plan built around your material.
- **Personalised quizzes**: Gemini writes practice questions from your own notes, so you can test yourself before exams.
- **Performance tracker**: shows which topics you have mastered and which need more work.
- **Student hub**: one place for all your courses and study material.
- **Guided Learning**: Gemini explains a concept step by step instead of just giving you the answer.
- **Deep Research**: Gemini reads many web sources and writes a report with citations. It's useful for assignments and literature reviews.

![Get more out of Gemini](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/gemini-more-tools.png)

- **Gemini Live**: talk with Gemini out loud to work through a hard topic or practise for a viva.
- **Image generation**: make diagrams and visuals for presentations.
- **Connected apps**: use Gemini inside Gmail, Docs and Drive.

### Who is eligible?

Read these rules **before** you start. Most failed applications break one of them.

- You must be **18 years or older**.
- You must be **currently enrolled** at a university or college. Google verifies this through **SheerID**, a third-party student verification service.
- You must use a **personal Google account** (an `@gmail.com` address). **Your college's Google Workspace account won't work.**
- You need a **valid payment method** (a debit or credit card that works for online payments) to sign up. You won't be charged during the free year.
- The offer isn't available in Bolivia, Albania, Canada, Hong Kong, Macau or Tunisia. **Nepal is eligible.**
- If your 2025 student AI Pro trial has ended, you can claim this offer too.

### Step-by-Step: How to Claim Gemini's Student Plan 📝

#### Step 1: Prepare your documents

Have these ready before you start:

- Your **college email address**, if your college gives you one (for example, `name@college.edu.np`). This is the fastest way to get verified.
- If you don't have a college email, a clear photo of a document that shows your **name, your college and the current academic year**. Your student ID card, admission letter, fee receipt or enrollment letter will work.
- A **debit or credit card that works for online payments**. Many Nepali bank cards need online or international payments turned on first. You can usually do this in your bank's mobile app.

**Tip:** If your document uses the Bikram Sambat (B.S.) calendar, put a B.S. to A.D. date conversion next to it in the same photo. This helps the reviewer see that you're currently enrolled. It's the same tip we give in our [GitHub Education guide](/explore/article/github-education-benefit).

#### Step 2: Open the official student page

Go to **[gemini.google/students](https://gemini.google/students/)** and click **"Claim a student plan at no cost"** (highlighted above).

Only use this official page. Don't trust "free Gemini Pro" links shared on social media.

#### Step 3: Sign in with your personal Google account

Google asks you to sign in. Use your **personal Gmail account**, not your college account.

![Google sign-in page](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/google-signin.png)

#### Step 4: Verify your student status with SheerID

After you sign in, Google One shows the student plan and sends you to the **SheerID** verification form:

1. Choose your **country** (Nepal).
2. Search for your **college or university**. As with GitHub, look for your campus name, for example *Birendra Multiple Campus*. If you can't find it, try the affiliated university, for example *Tribhuvan University*.
3. Enter your **full name exactly as it appears on your student document**, and your date of birth.
4. Enter your **college email** if you have one. SheerID sends a verification link to it, and many students are approved in a few minutes.
5. If SheerID asks for a document, **upload your photo**. Take it in good light, show all four corners, and make sure your name, college and the current year are easy to read.

#### Step 5: Add a payment method and start your plan

Once you're verified, add your card and confirm the subscription. The price shown should be **$0 for 12 months**. You won't be charged now. Google checks your card only so the plan can renew later.

#### Step 6: Start using Gemini

Open **[gemini.google.com](https://gemini.google.com)** and sign in with the same account. Your plan shows in your profile menu, and the student features are now unlocked.

![Gemini app home](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/gemini-app.png)

Try these first:

- Upload a semester's lecture PDFs to a **Study Notebook** and ask for a revision plan.
- Ask Gemini to **quiz you** on a chapter before an internal exam.
- Use **Deep Research** to collect sources for a project report. **Always open and check the sources yourself.**

#### Step 7: Set a reminder before the year ends

After 12 months, **AI Plus renews at US$4.99/month** unless you cancel. Set a calendar reminder for one or two weeks before the end date. To cancel, open **[one.google.com](https://one.google.com)**, go to your membership settings and cancel the plan.

Google also asks you to **verify your student status again each year** to keep access.

### Common Problems with the Gemini Offer ⚠️

- **"This offer isn't available for your account."** You're probably signed in with a college Workspace account or a supervised (child) account. Use a personal Gmail account instead. You also can't claim the offer if you already pay for Google One through another provider, such as a phone carrier.
- **SheerID rejected your document.** Upload a clearer photo that shows the **current** academic year. If one document isn't enough, put your ID card and your fee receipt side by side in one image.
- **Your card was declined.** Turn on online or international transactions in your bank app, or try a different card.
- **Your college isn't listed.** Try other spellings of its name, then the affiliated university's name.

## Part 2: Other AI Tools with Free Student Access 🧰

Gemini isn't the only option. Here are other tools Nepali students can use free, especially if you want to **build with AI** (APIs and tokens) rather than only chat with it.

### 1. Gemini API in Google AI Studio: free API key 🔑

**What you get:** a free **Gemini API key** for your own projects. You can build chatbots, summarisers, project features and more. The free tier covers most **Flash and Flash-Lite** models and **Gemini 2.5 Pro**. It's rate-limited, and you can see your exact limits in AI Studio.

**Who can get it:** anyone with a Google account. You don't need student verification or a card.

![Google AI Studio](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/ai-studio.png)

**How to get it:**

1. Go to **[aistudio.google.com](https://aistudio.google.com)** and click **"Get started"**.
2. Sign in with your Google account and accept the terms.
3. Open **"Get API key"**, then click **"Create API key"**. AI Studio creates a Google Cloud project for you.
4. Copy the key and save it in an environment variable or a `.env` file.
5. Test it from your terminal:

```bash
curl "https://generativelanguage.googleapis.com/v1beta/models/gemini-2.5-flash:generateContent" \
  -H "x-goog-api-key: $GEMINI_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"contents":[{"parts":[{"text":"Explain recursion to a first-year student"}]}]}'
```

**Important:**

- **Never commit your API key to GitHub.** Add `.env` to your `.gitignore`.
- On the free tier, Google may use your prompts to improve its products. Don't send private or sensitive data.
- Google no longer publishes a fixed limits table. Check your project's limits in AI Studio.

### 2. GitHub Copilot Student: a free AI coding assistant 💻

**What you get:** the **Copilot Student plan**, which includes **unlimited code completions**, a monthly allowance of **AI credits** for chat and agent mode, Copilot code review, and MCP support. You can't pick a specific model. Copilot chooses one for you automatically.

**Who can get it:** students verified through **GitHub Education** (13 or older, enrolled in a degree or diploma program). It stays free for as long as you remain verified.

![GitHub Student Developer Pack](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/github-student-pack.png)

**How to get it:**

1. Get verified for the **GitHub Student Developer Pack**. We've written a full step-by-step guide: **[How to Get GitHub Education Benefits](/explore/article/github-education-benefit)**.
2. After you're approved (benefits can take up to **72 hours** to activate), go to **[github.com/settings/copilot](https://github.com/settings/copilot)**. Copilot Student should be listed there.
3. Install the **GitHub Copilot** extension in VS Code or a JetBrains IDE and sign in with GitHub.

**Note:** GitHub paused new Copilot Student sign-ups in April 2026 and **reopened them gradually from 16 June 2026**. If Copilot Student doesn't appear yet, wait a few days and check again. **Copilot Free** works in the meantime.

### 3. Claude (by Anthropic): free plan and Campus programs 🧠

**What you get:**

- **Claude's free plan** at [claude.ai](https://claude.ai). It's free for everyone, including students, with daily usage limits. It's good for writing, explaining code and working through problems.
- **Claude Campus programs** for students who want to lead an AI community at their college. Selected students run a **Claude Builder Club** (undergraduates), **Claude Campus Conversations** (graduate students) or **Claude Science Workshops** (PhD students and postdocs). They get a **US$3,600 stipend** and **API credits** to build with.

There's **no individual student discount** for Claude Pro right now. If your university buys **Claude for Education**, you get access through your college account.

![Claude login](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/claude-login.png)

**How to start with the free plan:**

1. Go to **[claude.ai](https://claude.ai)**.
2. Click **"Continue with Google"** or enter your email.
3. Verify your phone number when asked, then start chatting. You can upload PDFs and images.

![Claude Campus programs](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/claude-campus.png)

**How to apply to Claude Campus:**

1. Go to **[claude.com/programs/campus](https://claude.com/programs/campus)** and choose the program for your level.
2. Applications open once a year. In 2026 they ran from **1 to 12 September**, and the program runs from September 2026 to June 2027. Bookmark the page so you can apply in the next round.
3. You need to be **18 or older** and have **work authorisation in the country where you study**. Anthropic also expects you to already use Claude well.

### 4. Azure for Students: US$100 credit for AI and cloud ☁️

**What you get:** **US$100 in Azure credit**, **12 months of popular free services**, and **65+ always-free services**. You **don't need a credit card**. You can use the credit on **Azure AI services** to experiment with AI models in the cloud, and you can renew it every year while you're a student.

![Azure for Students](/blogs_assets/free-ai-for-students/azure-for-students.png)

**How to get it:**

1. Go to **[azure.microsoft.com/free/students](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/free/students)** and click **"Start free"**.
2. Sign in with a Microsoft account, or create one.
3. Verify with your **school email address**. Microsoft sends a verification link.
4. Fill in your details and accept the agreement. The credit shows in the Azure portal right away.

**Note:** Student subscriptions often have **low quotas for OpenAI models**. If you can't deploy a model, try another region (East US or West Europe usually work), or request more quota from the Azure portal.

## Using AI Responsibly in Your Studies ⚖️

Free AI is a great study partner, but it can also hurt your learning:

- **Follow your college's rules.** Many exams and assignments don't allow AI-generated answers. If you're not sure, ask your teacher.
- **Check facts and citations.** AI can be confidently wrong, and it can invent references. Open every source yourself.
- **Use it to learn, not to skip learning.** Ask it to explain, quiz you, or review *your* code. Don't just copy its output.
- **Protect your privacy.** Don't upload other people's personal data, exam papers you aren't allowed to share, or passwords and API keys.
